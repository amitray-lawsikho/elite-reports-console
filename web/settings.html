Breach rules and wording

The build dialog opens on these and saves back here — the same rule as Leads Console, for all three reports.

A lead is breached when its follow-up is this many days old or more and it has not been called since — or, with no follow-up, when its last call is older than this. Never called with no follow-up is breached.
Less-dialled DNP — Call Not Connected / Call Not Picking Up where
4 by default. "Equals" takes 3, 2-5 or 2,5,7.
Elite by default. Leave blank to open on every team.
Above this it reads "Dialled more than N times". A hand-set label such as "Dialled From Personal" is left alone.
